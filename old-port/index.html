The place

A little piece of the old city, made for today.

Old Port was born in Thessaloniki in 2018 from a simple idea: create a place where the day can begin with coffee, slow down over a cold beer and end with wine or a cocktail.

Come for the coffee. Stay for the night.

Relaxed service, a full bar and an atmosphere designed for long conversations — right in the heart of the city.
